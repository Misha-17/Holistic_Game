BEACON BAY - DROP-IN SOUNDS
===========================
Put a file with one of these names in this folder or in assets/audio/ (.ogg preferred, .wav or .mp3 also work).
The game uses it instead of the built-in sound. Missing files fall back to the old sounds.
Open the project in Godot once after adding files so it imports them.

MUSIC (one gentle loop, unchanged by emergency pressure)
  music_soft      warm pads / soft keys, gentle attack, no drums or alarm layers
The built-in 0.8.0 score is original 72 BPM ambient music, played at -20 dB
with a four-second fade-in and two-second fade-out. The older music_calm,
music_tense and music_panic assets are retained but no longer played.

SOUND EFFECTS (short, punchy, no long tails; under 1 s unless noted)
  new_call   a new emergency appears           - radio dispatch beep / pager tone, 0.5-1 s
  urgent     a call has under ~15 s left       - short alarm or klaxon burst
  tick       while a call needs crews and has <10 s - a single tick (played every second) or a ticking-clock loop (looped)
  surge      a busy wave is about to start      - siren swell or rising alarm, 1-2 s
  fail       a call failed                      - harsh buzzer / low hit
  rescue     a call was completed               - short positive chime / radio "all clear"
  select     you pick a crew                    - radio click / squelch
  dispatch   you send a crew                    - siren whoop or engine rev, 0.5-1 s
  arrival    a crew reaches the scene           - door slam / brakes / radio blip
  boost      coffee boost used                  - energetic whoosh or coffee-machine hiss
  click      UI buttons                         - small, neutral click
  win        shift won                          - short victory sting, 2-4 s
  lose       shift lost                         - short defeat sting, 2-4 s
  (optional) combo, upgrade, shift, work, alarm - the remaining built-in sounds
