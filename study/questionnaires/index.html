Workload questionnaires

Beacon Bay study. Fill this in right after each level. Works offline; nothing is sent anywhere.

Setup (researcher)

The level run ID is on the game's results screen and in manifest.json. With it, answers join the game logs exactly; without it they join by player role, level and time.

How did your workload change during the level?

Draw one line from left to right that shows your mental workload over the whole level. Left is the start of the level, right is the end. The middle line is your optimal workload. There are no right or wrong answers; draw what you experienced.

Mark the moments where your workload went up or down. Click on your line at that moment, then say what caused it. Add as many as you remember, or none.
